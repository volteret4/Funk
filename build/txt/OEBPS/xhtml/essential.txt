Essential Funk Recordings

The Funk Album is a collection of grooves, jams, ballads, riffs, and attitudes that brings all of the musical forces together. While anything can be played in a funky way, there are some records that are unique and comprehensive expressions of a funk philosophy, or are simply irresistible groove sessions that must be acknowledged. Albums in this list are graded as follows:

*** = Superior—Good grooves and good ideas, but could improve.

**** = Monster—Killer grooves, and a total listening experience.

***** = Classic—Must listen to it, or you’re faking the funk!

Some of the albums are diverse, while some are all-funk. Commentary explains the flavor of each record. Compilations and greatest-hits packages are not emphasized, and only referred to when an album extends the base of the artist’s catalog. Apologies to artists left off of this list. Albums available on CD in the U.S. market as of 1995 are noted on the right.
